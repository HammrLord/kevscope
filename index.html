kevscope streams traceprinter output in constant memory and tells you which thread missed its deadline, how late, and who was holding the CPU. Runs in your browser or as a CLI. Nothing is uploaded.

Drop a file here, or run traceprinter -f trace.kev > trace.txt on your target first.
